Whirl is a free, open-source reader for the Whirlpool forums (forums.whirlpool.net.au), Australia's broadband and technology community. It rewrites Whirldroid, the much-loved app that stopped being updated, for current Android.

Whirl is unofficial: it is not made by or endorsed by Whirlpool. You sign in with your own Whirlpool API key (Whirlpool → Account settings → API key).

<b>Read</b>
• Forums with favourites, group filters and page jumps
• Watched threads with Unread and All tabs, Recent threads, Popular threads and site-wide Latest activity
• Threads rendered natively, with coloured quotes, tap-to-reveal spoilers and links that stay in the app
• Tap a quote to go to the quoted post, even on another page
• Filter a thread to your posts, moderators, reps or one user
• Industry news, title search and user profiles
• Recently read pages are kept for reading offline

<b>Keep up</b>
• Watch, unwatch and mark threads read, with Undo
• Notifications for new replies in watched threads, checked at the interval you choose, with Mark read and Unwatch buttons

<b>Respectful of Whirlpool and of you</b>
• Posting, private forums and private messages open in your browser, where you log in to Whirlpool yourself; Whirl never asks for your password
• No ads, no analytics, no tracking; Whirl talks only to Whirlpool
• Free software under the MIT licence: github.com/dalbo/whirl

Light, dark, follow-the-system and "dark at night" themes; adjustable post text size; Whirlpool links can open straight in the app.
