# Plan: the archive reader (v1.8)

Decided by Dallin 2026-11-05 (`docs/plans/v1.7.md` §8 lists what this plan must cover); shaped 2026-11-08. Starting
measurements: PLAN.md §9 "Archive reader and in-app post filters".

## 0. Decisions (Dallin, 2026-11-08)
- **Reading mode**: Settings › Thread reading: **Pages** (today's view, the default in v1.8) or **Scroll** (one
  continuous list from the archive). The thread menu switches the open thread between the two.
- **Filters reach**: filter the posts already loaded (the last archive page, up to 800 posts, at first); a "Search
  earlier pages" button fetches the rest, with progress and cancel. Earlier archive pages are cached.
- **Reps**: built from a real capture (below), not left in the browser.

Facts:
- **Fresh**: the last archive page ends at the same post as `/thread/<id>?p=-1` (checked on 97q1vrm9 and 97r5wzn3).
- **Pages**: `#top_pagination li a[href]` in order; `li.current` is this page; `title` is the date range
  (`September 2026&mdash;November 2026`). The first page links by **numeric id** (`/archive/2747207`), later pages by
  `<slug>-N`. A one-page thread has no list. Multi-part threads list every part's pages, so follow the hrefs and
  never construct `-N` from a slug. 800 posts per page (fewer on the last, and fewer where posts are missing).
- **Missing posts**: moderator-hidden posts are left out (numbering gaps); there is no `.hidden` replyblock.
- **No moderator note** (`#top_notebar_public` is absent) and no `#watchbar`.
- **Moderators** by `.usergroup`: `Moderator`, `Section Moderator`, `Benevolent Dictator` seen. **Reps**:
  `.usergroup` `ISP Representative` plus `.username span.reptag` (the company). Other groups seen: Addict,
  Enthusiast, Senior Alumni, Alumni, Forum Regular, Participant, new here, Inactive Account, penalty box, Carouser,
  Merchant, Banned.
- **Size**: 1.1–2.0 MB decoded per page (served gzipped, ~10:1). `WhirlpoolHttp`'s read timeout already allows for it.
- **Every page says** "Spidering, indexing or crawling of any content is strictly prohibited without explicit
  permission" (an HTML comment at the top). Whirl fetches only what the reader asks for, as a browser would: no
  background or bulk fetching, earlier pages one at a time on request, cached so they are fetched once. Mention the
  archive reader in the note to the moderators (`docs/play-console.md`), alongside guest mode.

## 2. Parsing (`:core:scraper`, `:core:model`) — PR A, done
- `ArchivePage(threadId, title, forum, pages, current, posts)` with `partPages`, `pageInPart` and `pageFor(number)`;
  `ArchivePageRef(path, label)` with `startsPart`. Post parsing shared with `ThreadPageParser`.
- `Post.repOf` from `span.reptag`; `Post.role` (`MODERATOR` / `REP` / null) from the user group. Thread pages read
  `repOf` too, but nothing relies on it until a thread-page fixture with a rep exists.
- Tests on all five fixtures, including part boundaries and the exact mapping.

## 3. Data — PR B (`:core:data`, `:core:database`, `:core:network`)
Review 2026-11-08 (architect agent) reshaped this section.
- **One schema bump (v7)**: `posts.rep_of`; `archive_pages(thread_id, path, part_index, label, fetched_at,
  accessed_at)`; `archive_posts` with every post column, keyed by `(thread_id, reply_id)`, cascading with its page;
  and slim `archive_index(thread_id, path, reply_id, number, user_id, user_group, rep_of, is_op)` with an index on
  `(thread_id, user_id)`, kept for every page ever fetched, so filters are SQL and never refetch. One
  `MigrationTest` case.
- **Opening** (single-part threads): read the normal last page (`?p=-1`, ~12 KB, often cached) for the **last post's
  number** and the moderator note; `K = ceil(last / 800)`; fetch `/archive/<slug>-K`, or `/archive/<slug>` when K = 1.
  On 404, or when the thread has a parts notebar, fall back to the entry page `/archive/<slug>` and its list.
  **Offline**: the newest cached archive page of the thread, then cached `thread_pages`.
- **Refresh**: `?p=-1` again; posts numbered above the last archive post are appended from it (they are the same
  posts). Refetch the last archive page only when more than 20 new posts arrived.
- **The loader** (`ArchiveRepository.loadPages(range, onProgress)`), used by "Load earlier", find and filters: one page
  at a time, at least 1 s apart, stops on `Retry-After` (`WhirlpoolHttp` already parses it), cancellable, scoped to
  the view model. Each page: fetch, parse, write bodies and index rows in one transaction, drop it. A per-thread
  daily cap on searched pages (say 60).
- **Eviction**: bodies by page count (like `ThreadPageDao.evict` and `KEEP_PAGES`), least recently opened first,
  **never the open thread's**; index rows stay (small) unless the thread is untouched for 90 days.
- Tests: MockWebServer serving the fixtures (K = 1, K > 1, 404 fallback, multi-part, offline, cancel mid-load, the
  cap, eviction sparing the open thread); one Room test storing an 800-post fixture to see time and size.
- Fixtures still to capture: a 404 for a K past the end, and page 1 of a multi-page single-part thread.

## 4. Scroll reader — PR C (`:feature:thread`)
- Setting `threadReading = PAGES | SCROLL` (DataStore; Settings › Threads). Thread menu: "Read as one scroll" /
  "Read in pages" for this thread, kept in `SavedStateHandle`, keeping the post at the top.
- `ThreadRoute` gains the mode and a post-number target (its `page` counts 20-post pages), so quote links, deep links
  and watched opens land on the right post in either mode.
- One `LazyColumn` keyed by reply id. **Memory**: at most about two archive pages of `UiPost` (parsed bodies) at once;
  others read back from Room in one-shot queries as they scroll into range, never through a `Flow` on
  `archive_posts`. Process death restores the anchor reply id and the loaded page paths, not a list index.
- At the top an explicit **"Load earlier posts (page 57 of 58, January–September 2026)"** button (no auto-load:
  content jumping under TalkBack); progress and completion announced politely (`liveRegion`). Where numbers jump, a
  plain-text divider: "1 post not shown (hidden by a moderator)".
- **Opening position**, the existing `ThreadOpener` rules mapped to post numbers: watched at `LASTREAD + 1` (or the
  next post that exists, since hidden posts are missing), "open at last page" / recent / jump to last at the end,
  otherwise post 1 (as Pages opens page 1). Quote and deep links at their post.
- **Multi-part threads**: Scroll stays within the open thread's part; other parts through the existing parts UI.
- Moderator note from the `?p=-1` page as a header. Watched actions work as in Pages.

## 5. Find and filters — PR D (one PR: they share the loader, the header and "Search earlier pages")
- Find: v1.6's `TextQuery` over the posts with bodies in Room; the header says "in posts 45,601–46,125"; **Search
  earlier pages** runs the loader and matches page by page, keeping only matching reply ids.
- Filters: posts by **me**, **the OP** (`isOp` on loaded posts; today's page-1 lookup as the fallback), **moderators**,
  **reps**, **this user** (post menu), from `archive_index` in SQL. Replaces v1.1.2's browser hand-off. From Pages a
  filter opens Scroll filtered. Header: "12 posts by BRK in posts 45,601–46,125. **Search earlier pages**".
- Simpler fallback if C runs long: ship D on its own, showing filter results as a list while reading stays in Pages.
