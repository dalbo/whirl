We are now fully live in Google Play! Thanks for all the testers who have tried this out and given great feedback.

https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=au.howesthat.whirl

Testing users may still get some features before they are fully available. Right now version 1.8.0 is available to testers. This includes some new features based around reading the forum "archive" pages – scroll 800+ posts in a thread at once, filter by user within the app, and drag the scroll bar to move through the thread quickly. Also search within the thread in the app.

This can either be a setting to keep on all the time, or you can switch temporarily to read one thread. This does increase load time and data usage. But a few recent threads and pages will be cached locally to try and make it a bit more snappy.

Next release after that I'm looking at proper backup and import/export of settings so setup on a new device is easy.

Coming soon to the testers: draft replies in Whirl

Version 1.10 is nearly ready and will go to the testing group first. The big addition is a post writer: you can put a reply together in Whirl, then paste it into Whirlpool's reply box. Whirl still never posts for you (there's no login in the app), it just does the fiddly part.

  • Quote: long-press a post and choose Quote. It goes into your reply as "Name writes..." with their words, leaving out anything they were quoting. Quote as many posts as you like.
  • Write a reply is in a thread's menu, and once you've started one a Draft button shows in the thread's top bar.
  • A formatting bar for bold, italic, links, quotes, lists (nested too), spoilers and code, and a Preview that shows your reply as it will look on the site. Swipe between Write and Preview.
  • Copy and reply copies your reply and opens the reply page, ready to paste. When you come back, Whirl offers to delete the draft.
  • Drafts save as you type, one per thread. Drafts in the menu lists them all, and threads with a draft get a small pencil in the lists.

Also in 1.10: Settings › Backup can export your settings, favourite forums and muted threads to a file and import them again. Your API key is never in the file, so it's safe to share.

The rest of this post is a test, please ignore it. It's every awkward bit of Whirlcode I could think of, so I can check that Whirl's preview matches what the site really does.

Edge cases for Whirl's post writer (please ignore). Each numbered line says what it checks.

1. Unclosed bold: never closed
2. Bold closed on the next line: [*starts here
ends here
3. Misnested: bold both italic
4. Empty codes: *] and [//]
5. The same code nested: [*outer [*inner outer*]
6. Escapes: [*a*] and [q]
7. Dashes: a – b, a — b, a-b, a --b, a- b
- 8. A hyphen at the start of a line
9. Addresses then punctuation: https://example.com/path, then (https://example.com/a_(b)) and https://example.com.
10. A bare www address: www.example.com/page
11. At signs: d@llin and someone@example.com.
12. Not allowed HTML: <script>x</script> <b onclick="x">b</b> <u>u</u> <a href="javascript:x">j</a> 3 < 4 > 2 & AT&T & ©
13. A single line break follows
right here.

14. Two lines starting with $:

first code line
second code line

15. Too many structural codes:

> four quote marks

: two indents

      • * four bullets
    • two quotes and two bullets

16. A quote, a blank line, a quote:

first

second

17. A table with a trailing pipe and a pipe entity:

ab
c | de

18. A jump link with no text:
@76888415
