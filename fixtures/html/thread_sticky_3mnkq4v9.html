Bazzaman writes...

I turned off my router and Arris modem, waited 20 or so seconds and turned Arris modem back on. Waited till it got its connect back then restarted router. Speeds were back and webpages load fast again.

I think the equipment needs a power cycle every so offen just to refresh itself.

Hopefully someone with some serious DOCSIS 3.0 & Arris knowledge can answer, but will these cable modems change channels after startup or will it move to other channels in the case of noise, contention, etc.?

I've heard of multiple cases where bad speeds (including just upload) were due to corrosion at the tap and have heard of instances where the NBN installers put their test device on and find only some channels are impacted by noise. It is also entirely possible there is contention on the uplink, but I don't recall ever hearing of anyone hitting that other than sporadically.

Anyway, the point of my original question is that restarting the cable modem may have caused it to sync to channels that aren't as impacted by noise or interference. There is also a chance there was a transient issue in the router. It's not unheard of, but it's only been the FTTN people that seem to hit that kind of thing.

If you get the problem again, try restarting only one device and check things. Personally, I would do the router first because it's your responsibility and is a lot easier to replace than the pain of getting ISP's to convince NBN to show up or look into their network.
