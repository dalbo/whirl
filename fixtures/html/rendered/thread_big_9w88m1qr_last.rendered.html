We've finally replaced our phones with Pixel 11. The car (BYD Dolphin) has wireless charging (Qi2) but, as the case with most older in-car wireless chargers, these would be implemented using the older, Qi1 standard.

The issue is that Qi2 introduces MagSafe where magnets are used to align the phone and the charger coils for maximum charging efficiency. Qi1 obviously misses out on these. This creates two problems, one, the phone and case magnets can interfere with the car's FOD (Foreign Object Detection) and shut down the charging pad and two, the misalignment can stop charging altogether.

I'm hoping people can discuss potential hacks to get new phones still charging on older pads.

Some ideas:

  1. get a good quality MagSafe sticker and attach it to the pad – this can be tricky, the ring itself can't trigger FOD, if it does, it won't work; you need good adhesion as the magnets may pull the sticker off; plus you obviously you need to get the alignment right but, it it works, it should keep the phone aligned and charging albeit at a slower 5-10W speed
  2. get a separate Qi2 charger – this unlocks the spec's full 15+W
  3. get an aftermarket Qi2 compatible modules – the nuclear option, if available.

I've ordered UGreen Magsafe stickers to try the first option... I'm pretty sure I can align the phone in a particular spot that will charge – provided the ring doesn't trigger FOD.

Curious if anyone tried and had success with any of the above and perhaps some here may not have been aware why some chargers don't work.
