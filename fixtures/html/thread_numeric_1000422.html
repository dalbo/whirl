In the long run you're going to have to learn how to eat a healthy diet and have a more active lifestyle without buying special meals and such – managing the balance of food intake and energy use yourself. Hopefully Lite 'n' Easy is giving you assistance in this because their long term goal should not be to have you hooked but to help to achieve self-management.

I would suggest that you find some other sources of information too, as more education will help you to become independent of the expensive meals more quickly.

Restrictive diets aren't sustainable, so I don't recommend books of that kind. Instead, perhaps try reading this book which uses some really up-to-date science to explain hunger and plateau's of weight loss. I was very impressed by an interview I read with the author, a molecular scientist

Secondly, get some proper advice about exercise for helping manage Diabetes. This will be more valuable than going in blind or starting some generic gym program. Since your profile says you're in Victoria, I suggest contacting an accredited cardiac Exercise Physiologist in your area via the AAESS website.

Any lifestyle change is difficult, don't try to alter everything at once. You shouldn't be that hungry all the time, so persist with talking to L 'n' E until they do a more comprehensive analysis of your needs. Afterall, that is their job.

Tiedan,

Good on you for giving it a go I think you've done well so far and I encourage you to go further.

But I think if you have to make too big of a sacrifice in terms of your everyday life in order to reach you goal, it's very very difficult to keep up.

It's a bit like for example, saving water, you can cut down your shower time to say 5 minutes without too much trouble and save water at the same time. But if you are to only have an one-minute shower, it might work for a week but it would be too hard to keep up.

To me I think the (small) amount of food and the high price would be prohibitive for you to keep it up.

Do you normally cook at home? It may seem difficult and time consuming at first but it's cheaper and more healthier and can help you reach your goal as well. You can find tons of healthy recipes on the net or in the books. I'm sure there are ways to keep the calories down and keep you feeling full. $151 a week for a person is way too much.

I encourage you to cook for yourself and you can try to copy Lite n' Easy's recipes. Don't give in to the temptation of buying takeaways just because they might seem a more convenient option.

I hope it helps a it and let us know how you're going.
