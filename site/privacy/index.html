Privacy policy

Whirl for Android · last updated 21 October 2026

Whirl is an unofficial reader for the Whirlpool forums (whirlpool.net.au), written by Dallin Howes and published as free software. This policy covers the Whirl app. It is not made by or affiliated with Whirlpool.

The short version

Whirl has no servers, no accounts of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. The developer receives no data from the app. The app talks only to Whirlpool, on your behalf, using the API key you give it.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Your Whirlpool API key and the name and user number it belongs to. They stay on your phone and are excluded from Android's cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. Logging out in Settings deletes them.
  • Cached forum content: forum and thread lists, your watched threads and the last 50 thread pages you opened, so the app works offline. This is public forum content plus your watched list; Android may include it in your own device backup.
  • Your settings and favourite forums.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes all of it.

What the app sends, and to whom

  • To Whirlpool (whirlpool.net.au), over HTTPS: your API key with each API request (to read your watched threads and news, and when you watch, unwatch or mark a thread read), and ordinary page requests for forum and thread pages. Whirlpool's own privacy policy covers what it does with them.
  • Nothing to the developer or to anyone else.

Links you open, posting, private forums and private messages open in your own web browser, where your browser's and Whirlpool's policies apply.

Notifications

If you turn on watched-thread alerts, the app checks Whirlpool in the background at the interval you choose, with the same API key. Android asks for your permission before notifications are shown.

Children

Whirl is not directed at children. It shows the content of the Whirlpool forums, which are for a general audience.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page and in the app's source repository. Questions: open an issue at github.com/dalbo/whirl/issues.
